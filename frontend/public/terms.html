Terms & event access

One purchase covers one invitation and its editor through the selected event date. Payment is charged once; there is no recurring subscription.

Your event date

Choose your date and timezone at checkout. Editor access ends at the following local midnight. The purchased date is fixed and cannot be extended in the editor. Please check it before paying.

What is included

Edit the purchased design’s text, photographs, section colours, typography and event details. Preview before payment. Publishing and guest replies activate after payment is confirmed. An account is required to purchase and access your invitation across devices.

After your event

The editor becomes read-only and guest replies close. Your published link remains available as a keepsake while the service operates. This is not a promise of permanent hosting. Keep copies of your own photos and event information.

Your content and guests

Use only content you have permission to share. Invitation links can be viewed by anyone with the link. Do not include sensitive information. Guest replies are visible to the host through their account.

Support and purchases

For payment errors, refund requests, account assistance or data requests, contact the operator shown below with your account email and purchase details. Any rights provided by applicable law remain unaffected.
