Privacy & your celebration

We store account email addresses, password hashes, invitation content, purchase records, event dates and timezones, and guest replies. These records allow the editor, payments, publishing and RSVP features to work.

Accounts and recovery

Your password is stored as a salted hash. A recovery code is shown when you create your account. Save it securely; it is required to reset a forgotten password. Recovery invalidates previous sessions. Email verification and email-based password recovery are not provided.

Cookies and device storage

An essential HttpOnly cookie keeps your session signed in. Drafts and cached invitations are also stored in your browser. Signing out clears the local invitation cache on that device. No advertising or analytics tracking is included in this build.

Payments and media

Stripe handles payment details on its hosted checkout. We store payment references and order amounts, not card numbers. Google Fonts may receive your IP address when fonts load. If a host adds external photos or video, your browser connects to that media provider.

Invitation privacy

Public invitation links are unlisted, but anyone with a link can view the invitation. Guest replies are stored privately for the invitation owner. A session cookie lets guests update their reply from the same browser; it does not verify their identity.

Retention and requests

Event expiry closes editing and replies; it does not erase the invitation, account or purchase records. Contact the operator to request assistance, export or deletion. Records needed to resolve payments or meet applicable obligations may need to be retained.
