# Atelier collection

14 new invitations, one per existing occasion. Signature remains AED 79; Atelier is AED 199–299 before VAT. Prices flow through the existing shared catalogue, server quote, checkout and structured-data pipeline.

Design direction: collected luxury, with architectural portrait framing and editorial typography. DFII: impact 5 + fit 5 + feasibility 4 + performance 4 − consistency risk 3 = 15. Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, Cinzel and Jost use existing font loading. Editor colour tokens govern all invitation treatments. Motion uses Anime.js 4 timelines and stagger, completing on pause or reduced motion. No new continuous loops or WebGL contexts.

Atelier changes the composition and styling, not service entitlements. No concierge or custom-design service is implied. Prices are configured proposals in this code, not live published changes.

SEO/AEO/GEO: server-rendered design directory, factual collection comparison, per-design visible questions and matching structured data, dynamic product offers, sitemap and llms.txt catalogue. No ranking or AI citation guarantees.
