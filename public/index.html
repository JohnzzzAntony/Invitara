The modern invitation

What is a digital invitation?

A digital invitation is an interactive event page your guests open from one link or QR code. It holds everything a printed card does, plus your photos, story, schedule, venue map and online RSVP, and it can be updated whenever plans change.

A few helpful details

Digital invitation questions, answered.

Do guests need an app or an account to RSVP?

No. Guests open your invitation link in any browser on their phone or computer and reply in seconds. Only you, the host, need an account.

Can I share my invitation on WhatsApp?

Yes. Every published invitation has one private link and a QR code, so you can share it by WhatsApp, text, email or social media, or print the QR code.

How much does a digital invitation cost?

Each invitation is a one-time payment per event, starting from AED 79 plus 5% VAT. There is no subscription, and you can personalise a draft for free before paying.

Can I try a design before paying?

Yes. Preview any design and customize a draft for free. Drafts stay in this browser. Sign in and pay to publish and save your invitation to your account.

Who can see my invitation?

Anyone with your published link can view it. Share it only with the people you want to invite. Guest replies are visible to the host.

What happens after my event?

Editing and guest replies close at midnight after the event in your chosen timezone. Your published invitation remains available as a keepsake.

How do I recover my account?

Save the private recovery key shown when you register. You’ll need it if you forget your password; we do not send password-reset emails.
