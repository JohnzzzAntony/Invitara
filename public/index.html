A few helpful details

Before your first invitation.

Can I try a design before paying?

Yes. Preview any design and customize a draft for free. Drafts stay in this browser. Sign in and pay to publish and save your invitation to your account.

Who can see my invitation?

Anyone with your published link can view it. Share it only with the people you want to invite. Guest replies are visible to the host.

What happens after my event?

Editing and guest replies close at midnight after the event in your chosen timezone. Your published invitation remains available as a keepsake.

How do I recover my account?

Save the private recovery key shown when you register. You’ll need it if you forget your password; we do not send password-reset emails.
