FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a digital invitation?

A digital invitation is an interactive event page your guests open from one link or QR code. It holds everything a printed card does, plus your photos, story, schedule, venue map and online RSVP, and you can update it whenever plans change.

How much does a digital invitation cost?

Each invitation is a one-time payment per event, starting from AED 79 plus 5% VAT. There is no subscription, and you can personalise a draft for free before paying.

Can I add multiple events?

Yes. Add every moment of your celebration to the schedule, from the ceremony and dinner to the after-party, each with its own time and description.

Can guests RSVP online?

Yes. Guests reply directly on your invitation with their attendance, number of guests and a message. Every reply appears in your dashboard, ready to export as a CSV file.

Can I edit my invitation after publishing?

Yes. Change words, photos and details until midnight after your event date, and guests always see the latest version.

Can I share it through WhatsApp?

Yes. Every published invitation has one private link and a QR code, so you can share it by WhatsApp, text, email or social media.

Do guests need an account?

No. Guests open your link in any browser on their phone or computer and reply in seconds. Only you, the host, need an account.

Can I add my own photos?

Yes. Use your own photographs for the cover, your story and the photo gallery, and change them as often as you like before your event.
