How We Met

From Strangers to Soulmates

It all began on a rainy Tuesday in October. Michael was rushing to catch his train when he accidentally spilled coffee on Sarah's favorite book. What started as an awkward apology turned into a three-hour conversation at the station café.

Three years later, during a sunset picnic at the same spot where they first met, Michael got down on one knee. With the city as their witness, Sarah said yes to forever.
